Reminder times

One summary notification at each time (Central time).

Sync

Settings, tasks you added, your edits and check-offs, and deleted tasks follow your Chrome account to your other computers, when Chrome sync is on. Each computer reads Classroom itself.

Developer tools

Pretend date and time

The extension acts as if it is this Central time, and the clock keeps ticking forward from there.

Test tasks

Adds fake tasks in every state: green, yellow, due today, red, and turned in. Replaces any earlier test tasks and leaves your real tasks alone.

Send test reminder

Sends the reminder right now, without reading Classroom first. Like a real reminder, nothing is sent when no task is yellow or red.

Reader log

What the last Classroom refresh found, and anything it couldn't read.

Saved Classroom pages

Runs the reader on the pages saved in test-pages/ (assigned.html, missing.html, done.html, assignment.html). It reads them as saved, so groups behind "View all" show as partial. Nothing is added to your list.

Reset

Clears all tasks and settings.
