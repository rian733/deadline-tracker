Deadline Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Deadline Tracker is a Chrome extension that collects your Google Classroom due dates into one list and reminds you about them. It has no server. The developer never receives, sees, or stores any of your information.

What the extension reads

While you are signed in to Google Classroom in Chrome, the extension opens Classroom's To-do pages in a background tab and reads each assignment's title, class name, due date and time, link, and whether it was turned in. It reads nothing else, and it only has access to classroom.google.com. It does not read your grades, comments, files, or any other website.

It also keeps the tasks you add yourself, your edits, and your settings.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. Uninstalling the extension erases it.

If Chrome sync is turned on, your settings, the tasks you added, your edits and check-offs, and the list of tasks you deleted are also saved to your own Google account through Chrome sync, so they appear on your other computers. Assignments read from Classroom are not synced; each computer reads Classroom itself. Chrome sync is run by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can turn it off in Chrome's settings, and you can erase synced data at chrome.google.com/sync.

What is shared

Nothing. The extension sends no data to the developer or to anyone else, has no analytics or ads, and doesn't sell or transfer your information. Your data is used only to show your list and your reminders.

Permissions

  • classroom.google.com and scripting: to read your To-do list.
  • storage: to save your list and settings.
  • alarms and notifications: for the morning and night reminders.
  • side panel: to show the list beside the page you're on.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked on the project's GitHub issues page.
